# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the ZikoJS community a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

We value constructive discussion, technical collaboration, and respectful disagreement. Contributors of all experience levels are welcome.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate of others.
* Communicate constructively and professionally.
* Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
* Give and receive constructive feedback.
* Focus discussions on ideas, code, and technical decisions rather than individuals.
* Respect the decisions and guidelines of project maintainers.
* Help maintain a collaborative and welcoming environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, insults, or deliberately hostile behavior.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
* Publishing private or personally identifying information without permission.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or other community spaces.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all ZikoJS community spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories and discussions.
* Issues and pull requests.
* Community discussions and communication channels.
* Events or other spaces where someone is representing the ZikoJS project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through the repository's private communication channels.

When reporting an incident, please provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

* What happened.
* When and where it happened.
* The people involved.
* Any relevant links, screenshots, or other evidence.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

* Removing inappropriate content.
* Issuing a warning.
* Temporarily restricting participation.
* Removing participation privileges.
* Permanently banning participation in project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will consider the nature and severity of the behavior, its impact on the community, and any relevant context.
